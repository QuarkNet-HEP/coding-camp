## 📦 How to use these notebooks

Teachers can either:
- Click the links above to open the notebooks directly on GitHub, or  
- Open them in **Google Colab** for an interactive coding session.

> Tip: To open in Colab, replace  
> `github.com/.../notebook.ipynb`  
> with  
> `colab.research.google.com/github/.../notebook.ipynb`
